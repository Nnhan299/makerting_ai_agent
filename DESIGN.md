# DESIGN SYSTEM & ARCHITECTURE — AURA MARKETING AI

> Established according to **Impeccable** craft floor standards for enterprise productivity applications.

---

## 1. Mode & Experience Target
- **Surface Mode:** **Operate** (Productivity workspace, multi-agent marketing orchestration, content workbench, brand safety compliance).
- **Core Intent:** The interface disappears into the task. Instant legibility, zero decoration slop, deterministic workflows, earned familiarity.
- **Audience:** Marketing Leads, Creative Directors, Content Creators, Compliance Auditors.

---

## 2. Craft Floor & Anti-Patterns (Enforced)
- **Zero Kickers / Eyebrows:** Absolutely no small uppercase tracking labels sitting above headings. Headings carry their own semantic weight.
- **No Nested Cards:** Cards inside cards are banned. Containers maintain flat hierarchy with subtle borders and clear grouping.
- **No Emojis as UI Controls:** All icons are drawn from Lucide vector library with consistent 1.75px / 2.0px stroke.
- **No Pseudo-Metadata Badges:** Removed unnecessary labels like `SRS 3.1`, `Module CAM-01..05`, or `1 Queued`.
- **Restrained Color Vocabulary:**
  - Base: Crisp neutral surfaces (`slate-50`, `white`, `border-slate-200/80`).
  - Brand Primary: Indigo (`#4F46E5` / `indigo-600`), hover `#4338CA`.
  - Semantic Status:
    - Success: Emerald (`#059669`, `bg-emerald-50`, `text-emerald-700`).
    - Warning: Amber (`#D97706`, `bg-amber-50`, `text-amber-700`).
    - Danger / Critical: Rose (`#E11D48`, `bg-rose-50`, `text-rose-700`).
    - Neutral / Information: Slate / Indigo.

---

## 3. Typography & Spacing System
- **Font Stack:**
  - UI & Headings: `Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`.
  - Data, Timestamps & IDs: `JetBrains Mono, monospace` (strictly for SKU, metrics, timestamps, and compliance rules).
- **Type Scale Ratio:** 1.15 tight scale for dense operational dashboard.
- **Rhythm:** Generous separation between logical modules (16px, 20px, 24px), tight internal component grouping (4px, 8px, 12px).

---

## 4. 4-Stage Marketing Pipeline Navigation
1. **Phân tích (Market Analysis):** SWOT intelligence, competitor monitoring, buyer persona STP, deep AI strategic research.
2. **Chiến dịch (Campaigns):** Strategic briefs, budget allocation, channel orchestration, dynamic status filtering.
3. **AI Studio (Creative Pipeline):** 3-column AI workbench (Brief context → Social preview canvas → Real-time compliance check & 1-click safe rewrite).
4. **Quy tắc (Brand Rules):** Rule registry, strict keyword enforcement, claims validation, live compliance tester.
5. **Lịch đăng (Calendar & Review):** Month calendar grid, multi-channel timeline, 1-click publishing, post inspector.
6. **Hiệu suất (Performance):** Real-time ROAS, Ad spend, CTR, conversion tracking, automated export.
7. **Quản trị (Admin & System):** Multi-user role management, API keys, cache control, system health audit logs.
